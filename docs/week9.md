### Critical Design Review (CDR) 

The Critical Design Review is the final review of the design portion of a project (the 'point of no return'). Its purpose is to fully solidify the design; it should include a full pass over key design features, including low fidelity mock-ups of assembly with partially working subsystems.

The CDR is not intended to yield a finished or polished device prototype; it should design, build, and test all major subcomponents, showing preliminary work towards completing all system features.

The core design should now be frozen; further work should focus on final builds, reliability, polished appearances, documentation, and presentation preparation.
