## Hardware & Software


### Hardware

#### 3D Printers
*Overview....Prusa MK4S, with a 0.4mm HF (High Flow) nozzle.*{: .text-red-300 }

Reservations: There are ~24x 3D printers in The Mill, half of which are class-only, and half of which are open access. 
1. Class printers (12x). These are accessible only to students taking Mill-based classes like ours. These 12 class printers are moreover reserved exclusively for our class during our lab times. 
1. Open access printers (12x). These are accessible to all students, including you, on a first-come first-served basis. An exception is for our 3DP-focused labs on October 16th and October 23rd, when all printers are reserved for us.

#### Material
*We will use 1.75mm PLA filament... Note that PLA is the only material permitted for 3D printing in the Mill.*{: .text-red-300 }

*Labeling instructions with class and name, have example image. This is to trace our class materials; This will also allow the Mill staff to contact you if your print fails while you're away, so you know whether to re-run the print*{: .text-red-300 }
