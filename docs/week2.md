## Hardware & Software


### Hardware

#### Laser cutters
Laser cutter model: The Mill houses 2x Laser cutters, [ULS Ultra R9000](https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmmxufkij00hath0i7rym83nu?utm_source=link). One is configured as a CO<sub>2</sub> laser, the other is a dual CO<sub>2</sub> and Fiber laser. In general, CO<sub>2</sub> lasers excel at cutting and engraving organic, non-metal materials like wood and acrylic, while Fiber lasers are optimized for cutting metals. We will be using plywood, using the CO<sub>2</sub> laser, so either laser can be used.

Reservations: The laser cutters are reserved for us during our lab times. They will occasionally reserved for other classes as well; all other times are first-come first-served. You can call the Mill to check their availability or check the [Laser cutter schedule](https://reg.hfs.uw.edu/Facility/GetSchedule?facilityId=f5ee6377-94cc-4c95-8611-a42f9d3e62af) on the [Mill homepage](https://reg.hfs.uw.edu/Waiver/GetFacilityAccessWaivers).

USBs: you'll need a USB to transfer your Illustrator files from the Mill computers to the Laser Cutter. The Mill always tries to keep a large stash of USBs to use, but it might be useful to bring your own as a backup. 

#### Calipers
Calipers will be useful for checking dimensions of your cut plywood sheets. These are available from the Mill front desk. See the [Calipers overview](https://makezine.com/article/digital-fabrication/3d-printing-workshop/how-to-use-your-digital-calipers-7-tips/) from the Readings.

#### Mallet
A rubber mallet can optionally be used to gently tap tight laser-cut finger joints into place without damaging the material. This is available in the Mill.

#### Materials
Basswood plywood, 12" x 12" squares of 2.7mm thickness (thicknes may vary slightly). Course materials will be stored in the Mill Storage Room.

### Software

#### Onshape
We'll use Onshape, a browser-based CAD program, to design our models. You can use your own laptop for this.  

#### Adobe Illustrator
We'll use Illustrator to process our CAD files for laser cutting. The Mill has 18 Illustrator-equipped computers for this purpose; you don't need a personal Illustrator account. You can preview a demo on the Mill's online [Adobe Illustrator & Laser Cutter UI Tutorial](https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmmxufkij00hath0i7rym83nu?utm_source=link).

#### Laser Cutter UI
We'll use the laser cutter computer/UI to setup and run our cutting jobs. You can preview a demo on the Mill's online [Adobe Illustrator & Laser Cutter UI Tutorial](https://app.supademo.com/demo/cmmxufkij00hath0i7rym83nu?utm_source=link).
