Please bring your personal laptop for design exercises.

More information about Mill-specific resources are given on the page for the relevant weeks' activities, but some key details are listed below.

### Equipment/Space Reservations
1. We will gather in the Mill classroom at the start of lab. This room is reserved for us for all class times.
1. The laser cutters and 12 class 3D printers are reserved for us during all lab times.
An additional 12 general-use 3D printers are reserved for us during lab times on October 16th and October 23rd.

### Equipment/Space Use
You can use the Mill space at any time during their operating hours, and will be expected to do so outside of Lab times in order to complete assignments.
1. 3D printing and soldering are permitted during all Mill operating hours.
1. Laser Cutting is limited to when Mill staff are present. 
    1. The hours available for laser cutting should be posted to the Mill website two weeks in advance: [Facility Schedule - Perks & Recreation](https://reg.hfs.uw.edu/Facility/GetSchedule?facilityId=f5ee6377-94cc-4c95-8611-a42f9d3e62af)
    1. Students are always welcome to call the front desk at (206) 616-1162 to confirm the availability of the laser cutters before they come in
1. There will be 18 desktops available with both Adobe Illustrator and PrusaSlicer for your use. 
1. Class materials for 3D printing (PLA filament) and laser cutting (plywood) will be housed in the Mill's storage room. If it's locked, you can ask a Mill staff to open it for you.

### Mill Staff Support
1. There will be a laser cutting training/demonstration during lab of week 1 (October 2nd), and a 3D printing training/demonstration during lab of week 3 (October 16th). There are two staff scheduled to provide both demonstrations.
1. All other weeks, there will be at least one Mill staff member during lab to support laser cutting and 3D printing.

### Safety

You will be prompted in Week 1 to complete the Mill's [Makerspace Safety Training ](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScEyBmyDhlK1QcHp4j7lbIcjaUiC2BfExZhf6I2WAlNpaDv7A/viewform) and [Perks & Rec User Agreement](https://reg.hfs.uw.edu/Waiver/GetFacilityAccessWaivers). In case these links change, you canalso  access both from the [MILL homepage](https://reg.hfs.uw.edu/). You will also receive in-person trainings that include safety protocols for specific equipment, including laser cutters and 3D printers. There will always be a Mill staff present in case of an emergency.

More generally, ensure you always wear clothes fit for an active Maker Space:
1. Wear closed-toe shoes: Cover the entire foot completely; sandals and open-toe shoes are never allowed.
1. Tie back long hair and beards: Secure loose hair and facial hair so they cannot get caught in rotating or moving machinery.
1. Remove loose items: Take off dangling jewelry, ties, scarves, and loose-fitting clothing before operating power tools.
1. Use required PPE when instructed; in particular, put on safety glasses for soldering.

## Assignments

On the lecture of every week, we will issue an assignment (1-week turnaround). Assignments will consist of a design/fabrication Task and a Reading. The Task will center on the current week's topic, and the Reading will be on next week's topic.

For example, Week 2 centers on 2D design and laser cutting. The reading on laser cutting, assigned in Week 1, will be due on the Week 2 lecture. This is so that you can come prepared with questions, and are ready to complete the laser cutting lab that week. You will not be able to complete the Lab without having done the reading beforehand. For topical clarity, the Week 2 reading details will be listed on the Week 2 page under a _Readings & Preparation_ section, but you will be prompted to complete this in the Week 1 _Assignments_ section.
