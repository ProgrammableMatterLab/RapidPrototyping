## Assignments

Every week will include an assignment. Assignments will include readings, individual design/fabrication tasks, and group design/fabrication tasks. The readings should be completed in the week before the lecture so you can come to class prepared with questions. The assignments should be completed by the following week's lecture. 

For topical clarity, these will be listed on the page for each week under a _Readings & Preparation_ section and an _Assignments_ section. For example, for Week 2:
1. The readings listed on Week 2 are assigned as an assignment in Week 1, and are due by the Lecture of Week 2.
1. The assignment for Week 2 is due by the Lecture of Week 3.
