## Grading


As detailed on the Syllabus, the Final Project as a whole constitutes 45% of the class grade. This is broken down as follows:
1. Physical Device 30%
1. Website documentation 30%
1. Interim presentations 12.5%
1. Final Presentation & Demo 12.5%
1. Peer Review 5%
1. Individual Contribution Record 10%

### Device

The final project should at a minimum integrate skills developed during the class, incorporating at a minimum:
1. 3D design and fabrication (3D printing and/or laser cutting)
1. Electronics (inputs and outputs)
1. Microcontroller programming
1. Functional behavior demonstrating a clearly defined design objective

### Presentation & Demo

Presentations will be held in The Mill on the final meeting of class, **Friday, December 11**. Each group will have approximately 10 minutes to present their work and show a live demo of their physical device, plus time for Q&A. The tentative plan for each group is:
1. Presentation (8 minutes)
1. Live demo (2 minutes)
1. Q&A with class (3 minutes)

### Peer Review
Group members will _individually_ submit a Peer Review describing the contributions of your team members. The Peer Review grade reflects the _quality_ of the review _you_ write for each of your peers. 

### Individual Contribution Record
The Individual Contribution Record will be calculated based on your peers' assessment of your contribution.     

### Interim presentations/documentation

You will discuss your particular project idea with the instructor via Design Reviews to determine its suitability.
